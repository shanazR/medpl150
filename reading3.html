Reflection: Motherhood in Crisis

Women in West Africa, particularly in the nation of Sierra Leone, are in desperate need of proper healthcare and utilities, especially regarding childbirth and labor. Many women are faced with improper treatment during childbirth without proper utilities and educated professionals. This causes various risks for both child and mother because a woman's body during pregnancy, labor, and postpartum is very fragile. The female body goes through a lot, and without proper care, this can be harmful. One of the stories shared is the story of Memunatu, who is from a village and did not have access to proper healthcare for her labor. A local “nurse” who was just an elderly woman with no educational background regarding childbirth. She takes the child out, leaving the mother in a condition where she doesn't stop bleeding, and that is not good for the female body. Such actions can mess with the uterus, a crucial part of a woman’s body. Memunatu was able to find a professional and get treatment; however, not all women have access to that, and that leaves women in pain, discomfort, confusion, and serious risks. Heela’s story explains how young girls are taken advantage of and used. Girls who go to school are used and then abandoned. Left pregnant and traumatized, leading to health problems of all sorts. No money, no family, no proper way of being able to take care of themselves. Heela was able to go to a center where they help girls, and she was able to get help and give birth in a hospital, eventually reconciling with her father, who was away from her. Heela’s health could have deteriorated without the help of the people from the facility. Kadiatu’s story with her children portrays the need for proper health care. Instead of relying on people who lack information, but that sometimes becomes the only option for many people especially villagers and people in these environments because they become so desparate for help they don’t know where to turn to. Kadiatu lost almost all of her children expect one, and because she had no access to healthcare she didn’t know what to do. With this child she was abel to finally have trust and save her other child’s life because there was help. She was able to go to the hospital and get proper treatment for her kids. Teteth suffered and piad consequences for a man who hurt her and their children. She had to pay a hefty pruce because she did something wrong out of defense whereas whne she was being hurt and torn, everything was going on fine. Women suffer in these conditions and to think that when welcoming new life should be filled with happiness and gratitude, many out there aren’t able to celebrate because of the burdens it puts on women. Women can’t properly take care of their children, or of themselves because of the lack of resources they have access to. They lack relaible resources and systematic laws to stabilize communities and these people.
